# Three operational expressions

## Mode A: Cinematic experience
Use for immersive portfolio, brand story, premium campaign, selective clinic hero or creative showcase.
- Objective: memorability, emotional resonance, clear conversion.
- Information architecture: concise high-impact overview; content-led chapters; tangible proof; frictionless CTA.
- Composition: a strong visual thesis, editorial hierarchy, atmospheric media, surprising but coherent section rhythms.
- Motion: deliberate sequence and scroll-linked narrative at key points, not universal animations.
- Accessibility: skip story intros, allow immediate content access; reduced-motion static experience fully functional.
- Guardrail: impressive visuals must not hide pricing, contact, service information, evidence or actual navigation.

## Mode B: Precision product
Use for POS, SaaS, admin, analytics, research repository, complex forms and dashboards.
- Objective: throughput, comprehension, accuracy, recovery, trust.
- Information architecture: task-based navigation, visible status, sensible progressive disclosure, dense but orderly tables/lists.
- Composition: modular grid, typography and spacing hierarchy, reduced decoration.
- Motion: immediate feedback, state continuity, small transitions only; no smooth scrolling layer by default.
- Accessibility: efficient keyboard workflow, clear errors, real focus rings, screen reader descriptions, robust zoom.
- Guardrail: aesthetics never reduce scanability, data density, form completion speed or exception visibility.

## Mode C: Hybrid
Use for businesses needing exceptional marketing plus functional portals (clinic marketing + booking, research project marketing + exploration, SaaS home + app).
- Public/marketing layer follows Mode A selectively.
- User/application layer follows Mode B.
- Bridge them with consistent type philosophy, color roles, icon/shape grammar and purposeful motion — not identical layouts.
- Design both contexts separately, then audit cross-context recognition.

## Domain calibrations
- Dental/medical: trust, evidence, hygiene, professional imagery, easy phone/booking, service clarity, accessibility, accurate medical claims; restrained atmospheric effects.
- Research collaboration: reliable navigation, member and paper discovery, credible identity, multilingual Sorani/English with RTL-aware composition, readable metadata, privacy cues.
- POS/retail: low-latency interaction, clear errors, high contrast amounts, split views, keyboard shortcuts, undo/recovery, touch size, offline/state behavior when relevant.
- Portfolio/cinematic art: highly conceptual narrative allowed, with accessible/fast path to work and contact.

## Intensity selection
For each project, score 1–5 for: task criticality, information density, emotion/story importance, latency sensitivity, content complexity. The higher task criticality/density/latency, the less expressive animation should interrupt tasks; the higher emotion/story importance, the more a controlled cinematic narrative is justified. This is a heuristic, not a mathematical decision engine.
