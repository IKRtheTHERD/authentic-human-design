# Project-specific calibrations

These are conceptual examples reflecting prior design directions, **not claims about inspected repositories or finished interfaces**.

## ARSCN — research cooperation platform
Mode: hybrid. Public landing page: serious editorial design, identity rooted in networks of scientific collaboration and readable research imagery, not generic mountain/particle motifs. Explore portal: strong discovery controls, metadata, disciplines, membership, privacy and clarity. Support English and Kurdish Sorani with tested RTL. Brand trust and legitimate research information take precedence over immersive effects. A measured subtle network transition may connect marketing to exploration, but not obstruct search.

## POS EHM — multi-tenant point of sale
Mode: product. Fast category and catalog navigation, legible prices, clear cart/tax/discount states, receipt preview, undo/recovery, touch-appropriate controls and keyboard workflows. Theme customization does not undermine contrast, predictable status or performance. No cinematic intros, unnecessary smooth-scroll or delayed state transitions. Visual refinement comes from proportions, density and reliable actions.

## Beyond the Fog — immersive narrative
Mode: cinematic experience. Darkness, fog, lighting changes, spatial perspective and measured pacing express isolation → decision → peace. Scroll/timeline choreography must be planned scene-by-scene. Preserve story continuity, skip access, full reduced-motion alternative and predictable loading. Atmospheric depth is justified here; it is not a default for unrelated products.

## Premium dental clinic
Mode: hybrid. Refined medical trust, credible real facilities/providers, clean typography, carefully art-directed photography, subtle parallax where useful, clear services, booking and contact. Avoid 'futuristic dentistry' clichés, fake testimonials and any visual spectacle that delays an appointment. Distinguish informational service pages from expressive home section. Ensure factual consent/medical claims.

## Personal portfolio
Mode: cinematic experience or hybrid. Begin with a specific point of view, evidence of craft and coherent case-study narratives. Hero should demonstrate a design concept; case studies should show problem, constraints, contribution, execution and measured outcomes where available. Make work and contact discoverable without waiting for a showreel. Prioritize meaningful evidence over generic agency slogans.
